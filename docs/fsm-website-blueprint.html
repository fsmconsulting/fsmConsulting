FSM Consulting Limited · Website strategy & information architecture

Website Blueprint

The site exists to make one person comfortable: the procurement officer, project manager or bid lead who has just seen FSM's name on a shortlist, consortium list or CV and has five minutes to decide whether FSM is real, capable and safe to put forward.

Built from the FSM Corporate Profile (r1-1), World Bank consultant-selection guidelines, the structure of established development consultancies (DAI, Itad) and professional-services buyer research. Current site design deliberately ignored.

01 · What the research says

How firms like FSM actually get chosen

Buyers eliminate before they call

Hinge Research finds roughly 9 in 10 buyers rule out firms before any conversation, and buyers almost always check the website, discarding even referrals that disappoint. For FSM the website is a filter it must pass, not a lead generator.

Shortlists are built on firm evidence

Under World Bank consultant rules, shortlisting weighs years in business, relevant experience, and technical and managerial capability of the firm. Key experts' CVs are not scored at that stage. The site must prove firm-level capability.

Peers lead with work, not slogans

DAI and Itad structure around five items: who we are, what we do / our work, insights, work with us, contact. Itad proves itself with a project portfolio and hard counts (projects, clients, countries). Careers and expert rosters are first-class paths.

Expertise beats polish

Buyers weight specialised expertise more each year. Published, practical knowledge (field notes, methods, explainers on IVA or FRILIA) signals a serious player far more than visual design does.

02 · Positioning

One sentence, used everywhere

FSM helps development-financed projects in Africa get delivered and verified — independent assurance, rapid expert mobilisation and hands-on delivery support, with field reach across all 36 states of Nigeria and a growing regional network.

The profile currently uses four different identities ("Development Delivery Partner", "Delivery and Intelligence platform", "Development Effectiveness firm", "Professional Contracting Partner"). The site picks one and never deviates. Tagline to keep: Global expertise · Regional access · Local capability · Measurable results.

Pillar 1

Verify

Independent assurance: IVA, third-party monitoring, audits, fiduciary and procurement reviews. Strongest proof point: nationwide field reach and independence.

Pillar 2

Mobilise

The right experts in the field fast: sourcing, contracting, surge capacity, visas, security, logistics, enumerators. The consortium-partner hook.

Pillar 3

Deliver

Getting projects ready and moving: D-Ready, PMO and delivery units, turnaround, MEAL, data systems, institutional reform.

Primary navigation (6 items): About · Services · Approach · Experience · Insights · Contact. Top-right utility links: Partner with us, Join our experts, and a persistent Capability statement button. Sectors sit inside the Services mega-menu.

Why 14 services collapse under 3 pillars: the profile lists 14 services with equal weight, which reads as "we do everything". Grouping them as Verify / Mobilise / Deliver gives a visitor one decision at a time and keeps every service findable for search and for EOI matching.

05 · Homepage

Section by section

  1. Hero. The positioning sentence as headline, one-line proof beneath ("Field teams across 36 states + FCT · Regional partners in Senegal, Sierra Leone, Cameroon, Kenya, Uganda"). Two buttons: Request a briefing and Download capability statement.
  2. Three pillars. Verify · Mobilise · Deliver, each with 3–4 named services and a link. This is the whole firm in one glance.
  3. Who we serve. Four doorways: IFIs & development partners · Governments · Private sector & investors · International firms. Each links to a filtered view. Worded as "we serve", never as client logos unless contracted.
  4. Why FSM (proof, not adjectives). Field reach map of Nigeria, independence statement, multidisciplinary roster count (only a real number), mobilisation speed (only if measured).
  5. Impact 360. The six-step method as a compact diagram linking to /approach. Shows FSM has a way of working, which reassures evaluators.
  6. Experience. 3 verifiable assignments, or, pre-track-record, "Our team's experience" with named leaders and the programmes they worked on.
  7. Leadership strip. CEO photo and two-line bio, board directors by discipline. People are the credential for a young firm.
  8. Latest insight. One or two practical pieces. Signals active expertise.
  9. Split CTA band. "Commissioning work?" → briefing. "Bidding in Africa?" → partner. "Expert?" → join the network.
  10. Footer. Registration number, Abuja address, corporate email only, LinkedIn, integrity & fraud notice links.
06 · Page specifications

What every page must do

Launch needed for go-live · Phase 2 after launch

Procurement pack Launch

/procurement
Job
Answer every due-diligence question on one page so an officer can complete a vendor check without emailing FSM.
Contains
  • Legal name, CAC registration number, year of incorporation, TIN (as FSM chooses to disclose)
  • Capability statement PDF (2 pages) and full corporate profile PDF
  • Policies as downloads: anti-corruption, safeguarding, data protection, gender, conflict of interest, QA
  • Leadership summary, office address, bank reference availability "on request"
  • Registration on UNGM / World Bank / AfDB vendor portals once done
CTA
Download pack · Contact business development
Why
No peer site does this well. For a firm without a long track record, being the easiest vendor to clear is a real advantage.

Services hub + 14 service pages Launch

/services/*
Job
Let a TTL or bid lead confirm FSM does exactly the thing their ToR asks for, in their own vocabulary (IVA, TPM, ESMP, FRILIA, D-Ready).
Template
  • The problem this solves, in the client's terms
  • What FSM delivers (the profile's service list)
  • How it works: 4–5 steps, deliverables, typical timeline
  • Who it's for (IFI / government / private)
  • Relevant experience or team credentials
  • Related services and sectors
  • CTA: request a briefing on this service
Priority
Write Project Assurance, Expert Mobilisation, Assignment Support, Implementation and MEAL in full first. These are FSM's differentiators.

Partner with us Launch

/partner-with-us
Job
Convert international firms bidding in Nigeria and West Africa into consortium partners or subcontracting clients.
Contains
  • What FSM brings to a bid: local registration, nationwide field teams, national experts, safeguards & MEAL depth
  • The cross-border delivery model diagram (client → technical leadership → experts → country access → mobilisation → field → QA → results)
  • Assignment support menu: visas, invitation letters, airport, accommodation, transport, security, translation, enumerators
  • Teaming models: JV, sub-consultant, local partner, mobilisation-only
  • Countries of access, labelled honestly as partnership, not office
CTA
Partnership enquiry form (firm, opportunity, country, deadline)

Join the expert network Launch

/experts
Job
Grow the roster FSM needs for every bid, and signal to clients that the network is real.
Contains
What assignments look like, disciplines sought (the profile's 14 expert areas), how vetting works, data-protection promise, form with discipline, country, years, CV upload.
Note
Pair with a recruitment-fraud notice. Development firms are commonly impersonated in fake job offers.

About, Leadership, Integrity, Network Launch

/about/*
About
Executive summary, vision, mission, values, the three pillars, a short "why FSM exists" story. No stat blocks that aren't sourced.
Leadership
Photo, full bio, programmes worked on and LinkedIn for the CEO and every director. Fill the vacant Fiduciary seat or omit it. Add a Global Key Experts section when names are cleared.
Integrity
Independence and conflict-of-interest stance (critical for IVA/TPM work), policy list, certification, whistleblowing contact.
Network
Nigeria map with 36 states + FCT coverage; regional map with partnership countries; the four access channels (direct presence, consortium, professional network, knowledge exchange). Honest labels throughout.

Experience Launch

/experience
Job
The page evaluators look at hardest. Show only what can be verified.
Format
Filterable list: assignment, client/funder (with permission), country, service, year, role (lead / partner / sub), outcome in one line. Detail page: challenge, what FSM did, result.
Before a track record exists
Title it "Our team's experience" and list programmes leaders and key experts delivered in previous roles, clearly attributed as individual experience. Never show funder logos implying FSM contracts.

Approach Launch

/approach
Contains
Impact 360™ (Identify → Measure → Plan → Accelerate → Check → Track), "results not deliverables" questions from the profile, evidence-based field verification, the quality assurance framework.

Contact Launch

Job
Route enquiries so the right person answers quickly.

Enquiry type selector (commission work · partnership · expert · media · other), Abuja address and map, corporate email only (retire the Gmail address), phone, LinkedIn, expected response time.

07 · Credibility rules

What the site must never do

  • Show funder or client logos unless FSM holds or held a contract and has permission. "Target clients" in the profile are targets, not clients.
  • Call partnership countries "offices". The only address is Abuja.
  • Publish numbers that aren't sourced (project counts, expert counts, "Pan-African hubs").
  • Present team members' past work as firm experience without attribution.
  • Use a Gmail address, placeholder names, or an empty board seat.
  • Let design outrun substance. Institutional buyers read polish without proof as a warning sign.
08 · Content FSM must supply

Blocking inputs

For launch

  • CAC registration details and incorporation year
  • Policies as PDFs (8 listed in the profile)
  • Leadership photos, full bios, LinkedIn URLs
  • Fiduciary director name or decision to omit
  • List of completed or ongoing assignments, with client permission status
  • Corporate email for all contacts
  • 2-page capability statement

For phase 2

  • Key expert names and consent
  • Real roster size and discipline split
  • Partner firm names per country (with consent)
  • Sample (redacted) deliverables: a TPM report cover, a dashboard screenshot
  • Photography from actual field work
  • Vendor portal registrations (UNGM, World Bank, AfDB)
